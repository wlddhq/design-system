## When to use

- A list, table, inbox or search with no results.

## Do

- One bold word or two: "Clear", "No campaigns", "Locked".
- An icon that matches the module.
- One action when there's an obvious next step: "New campaign".

## Don't

- No "You're all caught up!" or emoji.
- No paragraph explaining why it's empty.
