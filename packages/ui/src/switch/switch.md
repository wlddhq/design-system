---
name: Switch
component: Switch
summary: Turns a setting on or off, right away.
status: stable
group: primitives
---

## When to use

- Settings and display options that apply the moment they change: "Show completed", "Email me".

## Do

- Put the label on the left and the switch on the right of the row.
- Give it a `label` even when the visible text sits beside it.

## Don't

- Don't use a switch where the change needs a Save button. Use a Checkbox.
- Don't use a switch to pick between two named options. Use Segmented.
